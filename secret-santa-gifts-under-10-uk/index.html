Secret Santa gifts under £10 (UK): 14 useful ideas that do not feel cheap

Written 5 October 2026 by Tidy Ledger UK guides, an independent small UK publisher.

A £10 Secret Santa limit is tight, especially when you have drawn someone you hardly know. Below: how to choose, how to keep to the budget once delivery is included, and 14 gift types that tend to land well in UK offices, friend groups and families.

Quick answer

  • Do not know them? Go practical: a desk plant, notebook or phone stand.
  • Winter exchange? Hand warmers, bed socks or hand cream are hard to get wrong.
  • Party on the day? A pocket card game gets opened and used straight away.

How to choose a Secret Santa gift under £10

  1. Stick to the rules. If the group set £10, spend about £10. Going far over can make others feel awkward.
  2. Useful beats funny. Jokes about age, looks or drinking can go wrong in a workplace. A small useful thing rarely does.
  3. Avoid allergy traps. Food, sweets and strongly scented products are risky if you do not know the person. Unscented or non-edible is safer.
  4. Think about where they will use it. Desk, commute, kitchen or sofa – picking a setting narrows the choice fast.
  5. Ask a neutral question. “Tea or coffee?” or “cold hands or warm hands?” tells you a lot without giving the game away.

Budget tips that keep it under £10

  • Include delivery in the £10. A cheap item with paid delivery can end up over budget. Check the total at checkout.
  • Check the delivery date against your exchange date. Leave a few days of slack in December, when post is busy.
  • Multipacks can cover several exchanges. If you are in more than one Secret Santa (work, friends, family), a multipack split up can bring the cost per gift down. Check you are happy giving the same thing twice.
  • Wrap cheaply. Brown paper and string, or reused gift bags, look good and leave more of the £10 for the gift.
  • Keep the order confirmation in case the item arrives damaged or you need to return it.

Secret Santa gifts to skip

  • Alcohol – not everyone drinks, and it is not suitable for every workplace.
  • Strongly scented candles or diffusers for someone you do not know (allergies, asthma, shared offices).
  • “Rude” joke gifts at work – the laugh is short, the awkwardness lasts.
  • Gift cards – fine in theory, but a £10 card can feel like you did not try.

FAQ

What is a good Secret Santa gift for someone you do not know?

Something practical and neutral: a desk plant, a notebook, a phone stand, hand warmers or bed socks. Avoid food, scent and jokes.

Is £10 enough for a decent Secret Santa gift in the UK?

Yes, if you keep it simple and count delivery in the £10. Small practical items usually feel more thoughtful than a cheap version of something big.

Should I include a gift receipt?

If you can, yes. It costs nothing and makes it easy to swap a size or colour.
