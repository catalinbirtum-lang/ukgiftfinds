How we make money

We sell spreadsheet templates on Payhip: Tidy Ledger UK on Payhip. They are record-keeping aids. They are not Making Tax Digital software, are not "MTD compliant", and cannot send anything to HMRC. Where a guide is close to a topic our templates cover, it says so in a labelled box.

Since October 2026 our gift guides, product picks, Top 20 and TikTok pages contain affiliate links to Amazon UK. Each of those pages is labelled Ad at the top and each Amazon link is labelled Ad. As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases. Nobody pays us to include a product, and we do not show prices, star ratings or reviews (Amazon prices change constantly: each product has a button to see today's price on Amazon UK). Product photos come from the brands' own websites. Our other guides have no affiliate links, and there are no display adverts, no sponsored posts and no cross-site tracking (see Privacy below).

Privacy: how we count visits

We use GoatCounter (goatcounter.com, run from Ireland; data kept on servers in Finland and Germany) to count page views, visits, where visitors came from and clicks on our tool and Payhip buttons, and which Amazon product link was clicked, on which of our pages, and the kind of site you came from (for example Pinterest or TikTok) or the campaign tag (we do not see what, if anything, you buy). It sets no cookies. When your browser loads the counter, GoatCounter receives your IP address and browser details; according to its privacy policy it keeps them only in memory for up to 8 hours to tell whether a visit is new, and does not store them. We send only the page address, the site you came from (not its full address), your screen width and, if present, the campaign tags (UTM source, medium and campaign). Nothing you type into our tools is sent. We use the counts only to improve this site and do not share them for advertising.

You can object at any time, free of charge: Don't count my visits (this stores one setting in your browser; the counter then does nothing). If your browser sends Do Not Track, we do not count you.
