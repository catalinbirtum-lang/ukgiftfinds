Glass meal prep containers (10-piece sets)

Glass is non-porous, so tomato and curry don't stain it and smells don't linger. Many borosilicate sets go from freezer to microwave or oven (the base, not the lid). The catch: glass is heavier and can break if dropped.

How it works

  1. Batch-cook, portion into the glass bases and snap the lids on.
  2. Fridge or freezer. Leave headroom in the box if you freeze liquids.
  3. Reheat in the base with the lid off. Check the listing for oven, microwave and freezer limits.

Who it suits, and who should skip it

Good for

  • Weekly batch-cookers and packed lunches
  • Anyone fed up with stained plastic tubs
  • Reheating in the same box (lid off)

Skip it if

  • You carry lunch a long way and want it light
  • Young kids pack their own lunch: plastic is safer to drop
  • You need lids that go in the oven (most don't)

Check before you buy

  • Glass type: borosilicate handles temperature changes better than standard soda-lime glass.
  • Lid limits: lids are usually not oven-safe. Check microwave and dishwasher rules.
  • Sizes: compare the ml sizes with your portions, and check the set fits your fridge shelf.
