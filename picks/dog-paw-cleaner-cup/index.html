Muddy paws after wet walks? Keep the mud at the door.

Dog paw cleaner cup

A cup with soft silicone bristles inside. Add a little water, dip the paw, twist gently, then towel dry. The mud comes off before the dog reaches your floor.

How it works

  1. Add a splash of water to the cup.
  2. Pop one paw in and twist gently, back and forth.
  3. Take it out and pat the paw dry with a towel. Repeat for each muddy paw.

Watch our 14-second animated explainer on TikTok @ukgiftsfinds.

Why it's on our list

Which? (1 Oct 2026) named a dog paw cleaner (the Tinioey) the most popular present in its best Christmas gifts for dogs. Which? also notes some dogs might not enjoy the sensation.

Who it suits, and who should skip it

Good for

  • Dogs walked on wet grass, fields or towpaths
  • Homes with light carpets or a long hallway
  • Small and medium dogs (bigger sizes exist)

Skip it if

  • Your dog hates having its paws handled
  • Thick clay mud right up the legs: a hose or a bowl of water may be quicker
  • Giant breeds: check that the largest size fits

Check before you buy

  • Size: measure across your dog's paw and compare with the cup's opening.
  • What's included: some come with towels, some don't.
  • Cleaning: look for a removable bristle insert.
