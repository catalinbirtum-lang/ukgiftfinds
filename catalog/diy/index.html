DIY gift ideas (48 gifts)

DIY gifts suit people who would rather fix it themselves than call someone in. The best choices fill gaps in a toolkit: a good tape measure, a stud finder, a set of quality screwdrivers or a compact cordless tool for small jobs around the house. Before buying power tools, check which battery platform the person already uses, because many brands share batteries across their range and a bare tool is only useful with the right battery. First-time homeowners usually need the basics more than specialist kit, while experienced DIYers appreciate an upgrade to something they use every week. Safety equipment such as gloves and goggles makes a sensible add-on. We leave out consumables like tape and fillers, which are cheap and personal, and list durable, branded tools that will still be in the toolbox in years to come.
