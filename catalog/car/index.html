Car gift ideas (62 gifts)

Car gifts are some of the most useful presents you can give in a British winter. A jump starter, tyre inflator or proper ice scraper can save someone a stressful morning, and a sturdy phone mount makes navigation safer and less fiddly. Before buying, check that the item suits their vehicle: jump starters are rated for engine size, tyre inflators need a 12V socket or their own battery, and mounts vary in how they grip the dashboard, vent or windscreen. Read the brand's instructions about storage, because some battery packs need recharging every few months to stay ready. Car-care fans who wash their car every weekend will appreciate a branded detailing product they would not normally treat themselves to. New drivers often have very little kit, so a small emergency set is a thoughtful first-car present.
