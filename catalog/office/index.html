Office gift ideas (24 gifts)

Office and desk gifts suit home workers, students and anyone who spends long days at a screen. The most appreciated items tend to make the desk more comfortable or tidier: a laptop stand, a supportive keyboard and mouse, a desk lamp with adjustable light or a well-made notebook. Before buying ergonomic kit, consider the person's setup, because a laptop stand only helps if they also have a separate keyboard, and a large mouse may not suit smaller hands. Check connection types too, since some wireless accessories need a USB receiver and others use Bluetooth. Stationery fans often have favourite brands, so a premium version of something they already use can be a nice treat. We skip bulk consumables like printer ink and paper, and list items that will be part of their working day for a long time.
