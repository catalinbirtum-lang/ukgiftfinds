Travel gift ideas (38 gifts)

Travel gifts should make a trip lighter, calmer or better organised. Packing cubes, a compact toiletry bag, a slim power bank or a comfortable neck pillow are the kind of items frequent travellers replace often and appreciate in good quality. Think about how the person travels: cabin-bag flyers need small, light items that meet airline rules, road-trippers can take bulkier kit, and commuters value anything that fits in a work bag. Check dimensions and weight on the brand page before buying luggage, as budget airlines differ in what they allow on board. Liquids and batteries have specific limits in hand luggage, so look for clear watt-hour ratings on electronics. A practical item paired with a note about where they are going next makes a personal, useful gift for someone with a trip coming up.
