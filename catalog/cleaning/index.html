Cleaning gift ideas (17 gifts)

Cleaning gadgets can be surprisingly welcome gifts, as long as they take effort away rather than add a chore. Cordless vacuums, handheld vacuums, window cleaners and steam mops are the kind of upgrades many households want but put off buying. Think about the home: a small flat suits compact kit that stores in a cupboard, a family with pets may need strong suction and tools for hair, and a house with stairs benefits from something light and cordless. Check run time, bin capacity and how easy the filter is to clean, all of which brands list on their product pages. Be thoughtful about who you buy for, since cleaning gifts can be read the wrong way; they work best for people who have said they want one. We leave out cleaning liquids and other consumables.
