Garden gift ideas (28 gifts)

Garden gifts make sense for anyone with a patch of lawn, a balcony or a windowsill full of pots. Keen gardeners usually have strong opinions about their tools, so a quality version of something they use often, such as secateurs, a kneeler or a hose fitting, is safer than a novelty item. For new gardeners, simple kit that helps them get started works best. Think about the space: a balcony gardener needs compact planters, while someone with a large garden might welcome outdoor lighting or a bird feeder they can watch from the kitchen. Check whether outdoor electrical items are rated for UK weather and whether solar lights get enough sun where they will be placed. Seeds and compost are personal and seasonal, so we focus on durable, branded items that last more than one season.
