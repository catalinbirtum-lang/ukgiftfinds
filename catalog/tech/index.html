Tech gift ideas (78 gifts)

Good tech gifts are the accessories people put off buying for themselves: a power bank that can top up a laptop, a charger with enough ports for every device, a decent keyboard for a home desk. Compatibility matters more than anything, so check the connector (USB-C, Lightning or MagSafe), the wattage the person's laptop or phone needs, and whether a case will block a magnetic charger. For travel, many airlines limit power banks by watt-hours, so the brand page usually lists the rating; keep it within carry-on rules. Brand-name accessories tend to have clearer safety information and better support than unbranded ones, which is why this catalogue only lists recognisable brands. If you are unsure what someone already owns, a general-purpose item such as a multi-port charger is a safer choice than anything model-specific.
