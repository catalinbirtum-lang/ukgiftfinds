Toys/Kids gift ideas (135 gifts)

Toy and kids' gifts are easiest when you match the child's age and current interests. Every toy listed here comes from a recognisable brand, and the age guidance on the box matters: small parts are a real risk for under-threes, and toys aimed at older children can be frustrating for younger ones. Building sets, craft kits and board games often hold attention longer than single-use novelties, and family games give everyone something to do together over the holidays. If a toy is linked to a film, show or trend, check that the child is actually a fan before buying, as these crazes change quickly. Batteries are often not included, so read the brand's description and buy the right ones separately. For birthdays and Christmas, asking parents what is already on the list avoids duplicates.
