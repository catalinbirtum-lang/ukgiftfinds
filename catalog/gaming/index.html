Gaming gift ideas (58 gifts)

Gaming gifts are easy to get wrong if you don't know the player's setup, so start with the platform: PlayStation, Xbox, Nintendo Switch or PC. Games and many accessories only work on one system, and the newest consoles may not support older controllers. Check the age rating on the box, which follows the PEGI system in the UK, especially when buying for children and teens. Accessories such as headsets, controllers and charging docks are often safer bets than games, because players tend to already own the titles they want most. For PC gamers, look at connection type and compatibility before buying a keyboard, mouse or headset. If you are stuck, a quality controller or headset from a well-known brand is a gift most players will use. We list physical products and editions, not download codes.
