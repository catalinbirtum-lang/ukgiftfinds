Beauty gift ideas (146 gifts)

Beauty gifts are personal, so the safest picks are the ones that fit a routine the person already has. Skincare fans often have favourite brands and skin types to work around, while someone newer to skincare may enjoy a simple, well-known product more than a complicated multi-step set. Hair tools such as straighteners and curlers make strong gifts for people who style daily; check the plate size, heat settings and whether the tool suits their hair length. Advent calendars and gift sets are a good way to let someone try several products without committing to full sizes. Always look at the ingredient list on the brand's own site if the recipient has sensitive skin or allergies, and avoid anything that makes medical claims. When in doubt, a gift receipt is worth asking for.
