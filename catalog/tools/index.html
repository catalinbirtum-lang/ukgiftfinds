Tools gift ideas (35 gifts)

Tool gifts are best for people who already enjoy making and mending things. A quality hand tool, a precise measuring device or a well-organised tool case can become something they use for decades. If you are choosing a cordless power tool, find out which battery system they already own, as staying within one brand saves money and storage space. For general home use, a mid-range tool from a recognised brand is usually a better gift than a huge cheap set with many pieces they will never use. Look for clear safety guidance and a warranty on the brand's website. Beginners appreciate versatile basics such as a good screwdriver set or a multi-tool, while experienced makers often have a wish list of specific upgrades. Ask a partner or friend what is missing from their toolbox if you can.
