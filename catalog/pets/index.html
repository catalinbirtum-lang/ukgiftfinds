Pets gift ideas (68 gifts)

Pet gifts are really gifts for the owner too, so pick things that make daily life with the animal easier or more fun. Durable toys suit strong chewers, puzzle feeders help dogs and cats that get bored when left alone, and a good grooming tool saves time for owners of long-haired breeds. Size is the most common mistake: a toy that is too small for a large dog can be a choking risk, so follow the brand's size guide by weight or breed. For cats, interactive toys and scratchers usually last longer than plush toys. We leave out food, treats and anything sold as a health treatment, because diets and medical needs vary and are best chosen by the owner or a vet. If you are buying for a new pet owner, practical starter kit is usually welcome.
