Kitchen gift ideas (70 gifts)

Kitchen gifts are only good gifts if they get used, so start with how the person cooks. Someone who batch-cooks on Sundays will value a slow cooker or proper storage containers; a coffee lover will use a moka pot every morning; a student in a shared kitchen needs compact kit that is easy to wash and store. Look at capacity before anything else: a large air fryer is great for a family but takes over a small worktop. Check whether parts are dishwasher safe, because that one detail decides whether a gadget stays out or goes to the back of a cupboard. For hobby cooks, a single well-made tool usually beats a big set of novelty items. Avoid buying duplicates of things most kitchens already have unless you know theirs is worn out.
