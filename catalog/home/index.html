Home gift ideas (38 gifts)

Home gifts work best when they solve something the person already grumbles about: a cold sofa in January, a dark hallway, a bedroom that never feels quite cosy. Before you pick, think about the space they actually live in. A renter in a small flat usually wants things that need no drilling and pack away, while a homeowner may welcome something more permanent. Check the size and colour carefully, because a throw or lamp that clashes with the room tends to stay in its box. Electric items such as heated throws and lamps should come with a UK plug and clear safety instructions from the brand. If you are buying for someone who has just moved, practical upgrades often land better than decorative pieces, because a first home always lacks the boring essentials.
