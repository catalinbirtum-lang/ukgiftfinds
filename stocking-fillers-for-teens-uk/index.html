Stocking fillers for teens (UK): 14 ideas they will actually use

Written 5 October 2026 by Tidy Ledger UK guides, an independent small UK publisher.

Teenagers are famously hard to buy for, and stocking fillers add up quickly. Here is how to pick things they will actually use, how to keep the total down, and 14 stocking filler types for UK teens aged roughly 13 to 17.

Quick answer

  • Safest bet: a long charging cable or phone grip – check their phone first.
  • For the bedroom: photo clip string lights.
  • For revision season: highlighters or a book light.
  • Screen-free: a speed cube or group card game.

How to choose stocking fillers for teens

  1. Find out their phone model. Charging cables and phone accessories are only useful if they fit. Ask casually or check with a parent.
  2. Mix useful with fun. One practical thing (cable, pens) plus one fun thing (stickers, game) per stocking works well.
  3. Do not guess at skincare. Keep to simple, gentle products, and let teens choose anything “active” themselves.
  4. Check the age rating on games and anything with small parts if younger siblings are around.
  5. Avoid anything that needs an age check – for example energy drinks, knives or lighters.

Budget tips for teen stockings

  • Set a per-stocking total first, then split it across 3 to 5 small items, rather than buying item by item.
  • Multipacks for siblings: scrunchies, socks and sticker packs can be split between stockings.
  • Count delivery and batteries. A cheap item with paid delivery, or lights with no batteries included, can cost more than it looks.
  • Order early in December and check the delivery date shown at checkout before you pay.
  • Keep the order confirmation so a wrong size or connector can be returned or swapped.

Stocking fillers to skip for teens

  • Anything age-restricted (energy drinks, knives, lighters, vapes) – not suitable, and often illegal to sell to under-18s.
  • Strong “active” skincare chosen for them without asking.
  • Childish versions of things they have outgrown – if in doubt, ask a parent or older sibling.

FAQ

What do you put in a teenager's stocking in the UK?

A mix of practical and fun: a charging cable or phone grip, something for their hair or room, revision stationery, and a game or puzzle for the holidays.

How much should I spend on stocking fillers for a teen?

There is no rule. Decide a total that suits your family first, then split it across a few small items.

Are these suitable for boys and girls?

Most are. Tech bits, stationery, games and socks suit almost anyone; hair and beauty items depend on the teen.
