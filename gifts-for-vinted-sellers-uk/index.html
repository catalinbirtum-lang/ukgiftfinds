Gifts for Vinted sellers (UK): 14 useful ideas for resellers

Written 5 October 2026 by Tidy Ledger UK guides, an independent small UK publisher.

Know someone who sells on Vinted, eBay or Depop? The best gifts make the boring parts of selling – packing, posting, photos and keeping track – a bit quicker. Here is how to choose, plus 14 gift types for UK resellers, mostly aimed at around £30 or less.

Quick answer

  • Most useful for almost any seller: a digital parcel scale or a big pack of mailing bags.
  • For better photos: a phone tripod with remote or a small light.
  • For clothes sellers: a fabric shaver and a soft tape measure.

How to choose a gift for a Vinted seller

  1. Find out what they sell. Clothes sellers need garment care and hangers; book or homeware sellers need boxes, tape and padding.
  2. Look at their listing photos. Dark or cluttered photos suggest a light or backdrop would help. Lots of parcels? Packing kit.
  3. Consumables are a safe bet. Mailing bags, tape and labels always get used, even if they already have some.
  4. Think about space. Many sellers work from a bedroom or a corner of the lounge. Compact, foldable kit is usually better than big kit.
  5. Do not buy stock for them. Picking items for someone else to resell rarely works out – they know their buyers.

Budget tips

  • Pool together if a bigger item (such as a steamer or ring light) is over your budget.
  • Bundle small things: a tape measure, lint roller and thank-you stickers together make a thoughtful “seller kit”.
  • Check the total with delivery and the delivery date before you pay.
  • Keep the order confirmation in case something needs returning.

Packing and posting

The everyday kit every seller runs through. Consumables are never wasted.

Postage options are often chosen by weight, so weighing a parcel before you pick a size avoids guessing and paying for the wrong band.

What do you buy someone who sells on Vinted?

Kit that saves them time: a digital parcel scale, mailing bags, a phone tripod or light for photos, a fabric shaver, or clear storage boxes for stock.
